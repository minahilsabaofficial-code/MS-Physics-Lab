# [Project Name]: Virtual Physics Lab

A free, mobile-friendly virtual physics lab for Higher Secondary students, available in **English and Urdu**.

**Made for:** STEAM Exhibition 2026-27, Theme: AI, Digital Solutions, Gamification, Automation, Coding.
**By:** [Minahil Saba], [Government Degree Science and Commerce College Malir Cantt], Karachi

**Live demo:** https://[https://minahilsabaofficial-code.github.io/MS-Physics-Lab/].github.io/[repository-name]/

## The problem
Many government colleges have little or no lab equipment, so students often learn practicals only from the textbook. Existing tools like PhET are excellent, but they are mostly in English and not matched to our syllabus.

## The solution
Students perform physics practicals on their phone, with sliders and buttons, in front of a virtual lab. No installation, no equipment, and no cost.

## Features
- Experiments from the college physics practical manual
- Animated apparatus, with a ruler and a stopwatch
- Observation table and graph that fill in automatically
- Calculated result (for example, the value of g)
- Quiz with explanations and correct answers
- Answer key to check your experiment
- XP, levels and badges to keep students motivated
- "My Mistakes" page saved on the student's own device (no login)
- Available in English and Urdu Languages.

## Experiments
- [x] Simple pendulum: T ∝ √L and finding g
- [ ] Inclined plane: force vs sin θ
- [ ] Cell EMF and internal resistance
- [ ] Tungsten filament lamp: I vs V

## Built with
HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Chart.js. Written and tested on a phone, and published free on GitHub Pages.

## How to use
Open the live demo link in any browser and choose an experiment.
