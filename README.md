# Project Name: SciFyLab ( The Virtual Lab )

A free, mobile-friendly virtual physics lab for 1st year & 2nd year college students of Sindh Board, available in **English and Urdu**.

**Made for:** STREAM Exhibition 2026-27, Theme: AI, Digital Solutions, Gamification, Automation, Coding.
**By:** Minahil Saba, Anus Mughal & Ayesha from, Government Degree Science and Commerce College Malir Cantt,Karachi.

**Live demo:** https://minahilsabaofficial-code.github.io/MS-Physics-Lab/

## The problem
Many government colleges have little or no lab equipment, in result, students are not able to understand the practical properly. Existing tools and apps are excellent, but they are mostly in English and not matched to our syllabus.

## The solution
Students perform physics practicals on their phone, with sliders and buttons, in front of a virtual lab. No installation, no equipment, and no cost.

## Features
- Experiments from the sindh board practical manual.
- Animated apparatus, with rulers, stopwatches etc.
- Observation table and graph that fill in automatically
- Calculated result 
- Quiz with explanations and correct answers
- Answer key to check your experiment
- XP, levels and badges to keep students motivated
- "My Mistakes" page saved on the student's own device (no login)
- Available in English and Urdu Languages.
- Makes a report for the practical
- Lab Assistant to help students recheck their readings.
- helps students to perform and umderstand the practica;s.

## Built with
HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Chart.js. Written and tested , and published free on GitHub Pages.

## How to use
Open the live demo link in any browser and choose an experiment.
